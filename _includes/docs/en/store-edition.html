Differences between the Microsoft Store edition and the installer edition

The available features, accounts, and plans in the Microsoft Store edition are the same as in the installer edition (including subscribing to a plan, changing it, and canceling your subscription from the app). Only the following points are different.

  • Updates: The in-app update dialog and "File ▸ Check for updates" aren't available in the Microsoft Store edition (Updates)
  • Bundled third-party software: In the Microsoft Store edition, the package contents are read-only and signed, so you can't replace the bundled FFmpeg in place (you can still use FFmpeg that you obtained or modified separately under the terms of the GPL; see Bundled third-party software and licenses)
  • Premiere Pro integration: To use Premiere Pro integration with the Microsoft Store edition, you need to update the plug-in to version 0.1.21 or later. Select "Import into Premiere Pro" and follow the on-screen instructions to update it (Premiere Pro integration)
  • Uninstall: The confirmation screen has no check box. Your settings and sign-in information (%USERPROFILE%\.deepmosaic) and your project folders are left on your PC. After uninstalling, delete any folders you no longer need in File Explorer (note that deleting a project folder also deletes any exported videos saved in it)
