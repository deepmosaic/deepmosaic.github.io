If you are looking to remove mosaic

Deepmosaic adds mosaic to videos. It cannot remove it. We do not offer any product or service that removes, undoes, or restores mosaic, and we do not accept inquiries about that use. Installing Deepmosaic does not let you return footage that already has mosaic applied to its original state. This software has the exact opposite purpose of the feature you are looking for, so check this before you download it.

What Deepmosaic solves

Applying mosaic takes time in proportion to the length of the video, because someone has to watch the whole video and keep following the targets. In Deepmosaic, AI detects and tracks the targets throughout the video and applies mosaic, reducing the work people do to reviewing and correcting the detection results. Footage is never sent outside, and all processing happens on your PC.
