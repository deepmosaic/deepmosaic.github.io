Open Deepmosaic

Open the app with the organization code .

Open in app
  1. Select "Open in app".
  2. On the sign-in screen, check that the organization code is entered under "Enterprise plan users".
  3. Sign in with "Start with email" or "Registered with a password".

If the app doesn't open, copy the organization code and paste it under "Enterprise plan users" on the app's sign-in screen.

Sign in as an admin.

Open in app
  1. Select "Open in app".
  2. Sign in with the email address that received the invitation email (you don't enter an organization code).

This link isn't valid. Open the link in the email again, or launch the app and enter the organization code.

If you haven't installed the app yet

Get Deepmosaic from Microsoft Store. After installing it, select "Open in app" on this page again.
