Subscribe to a paid plan

You can subscribe to a paid plan from the profile menu in the app (if you downloaded the Windows version) or from this page with your Web app account. Either way, you can use your plan with the same account in both the Desktop app and the Web app. On this page, use a button below to open the Web app, sign in with the same account as the app, and pay by credit card.

{%- include store-subscribe-note.html %}

How to subscribe

  1. STEP 1

    Press "Subscribe" on a plan below, and the Web app (in your browser) opens.

  2. STEP 2

    Sign in with the same account as the app (if you don't have an account yet, one is created automatically the first time you sign in). Once you sign in, you go straight to the payment screen.

  3. STEP 3

    Once you pay by credit card, your account's plan is switched. In the app, just sign in with the same account and the change is applied.
